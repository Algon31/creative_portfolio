# Portfolio: Ravi Bhuvan

## Scope & Mode
- Target: index.html
- Mode: Experience

## Direction contract

THESIS:
A geometry-led creative portfolio that rejects standard developer grids and cookie-cutter templates in favor of an art-directed composition of floating squares, rectangles, and circles anchored by a rich terracotta field.

OWN-WORLD:
Strict three-color palette of terracotta background (#B75A48), light cream accent (#E8ECD6), and pure dark (#000000). Distinctive geometric rhythm, soft tactile shadows, and high-contrast typography hierarchy without extraneous accents.

STORY:
The visitor immediately registers Ravi Bhuvan as a multidisciplinary creative technologist and video editor through confident typography, animated roles, and an unconventional floating spatial interface.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
Full-bleed terracotta ground. Vertically floating left nav (40% viewport height, pill-rounded, smooth interactive tabs), top-right floating profile card with circular avatar placeholder and title, and an expansive center-weighted hero ("i'm" in dark, "ravi bhuvan" in oversized light cream, followed by smooth automatically rotating profession titles).

FORM:
Geometric Spatial Portfolio with floating UI controls.
seed: none (unavailable)

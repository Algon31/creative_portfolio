# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Vite + React / TypeScript + Tailwind CSS

## Users
High-growth tech founders, creative directors, and product leaders seeking high-craft digital experiences, interactive design engineering, and creative motion.

## Product Purpose
Serve as the independent portfolio and interactive showcase for a creative designer and creative technologist. The surface showcases interactive web experiences, creative coding, and motion design, demonstrating proof of craft to convert high-value client inquiries and commissions.

## Positioning
Unapologetically bespoke, craft-led interactive work. Rather than static mockups on a generic grid, the interface itself operates as a live demonstration of creative engineering, tactile physics, and refined motion.

## Operating Context
Evaluated in high-stakes commissioning and hiring reviews by founders and design leaders on high-resolution displays and mobile devices. Visitors scan rapidly for aesthetic distinction, technical fluency, and depth before exploring specific case studies.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- High-performance, 60fps animations and fluid micro-interactions.
- Modern frontend architecture with Vite, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
- Fast load times and responsive cross-device layout.
- Specific studio/designer name to be personalized (defaulting to independent studio identity).

## Brand Commitments
- Anti-generic aesthetic: avoiding AI-slop tropes (no default Inter, no uncalibrated purple/blue gradients, no nested card clutter).
- Visceral interactivity and purposeful motion.
- Confident, editorial typography and generous pacing.

## Evidence on Hand
- Interactive experiments and case studies to be built directly into the project.
- Real code and interactive artifacts (no fabricated client testimonials or fake metrics).

## Product Principles
1. **The Artifact Leads:** The interface is itself an exhibit of creative coding and interactive fluency, not merely a passive frame.
2. **Precision Over Noise:** Motion and interactivity feel physically grounded, deliberate, and performant—never disorienting or sluggish.
3. **Ruthless Clarity:** Navigation, project deep-dives, and contact actions remain instantaneous and frictionless.
4. **Restraint in Tone:** Confident, concise copy that lets the work demonstrate authority without hyperbole.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all high-velocity or ambient motion effects.
- Semantic HTML structure, full keyboard accessibility, and WCAG AA contrast compliance across themes and interactive states.
